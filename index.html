A church whose name has two parts

We are legally established as Gracepoint Prophetic Church. Our spiritual essence is The Jesus Home Church.

We move beyond the building. We hold that the human heart is the specific residence where the King of Kings desires to live, and every member is trained to become a “Home for Jesus.”

Our leaders and stewards carry one goal: to help each believer turn their life into a comfortable home for Him.

How we keep the house in order

Integrity of the Oracle

Prophecy is never sold, used for manipulation or used to bring shame. Every word passes through the filters of love, care and respect.

Financial holiness

Tithes and offerings are holy. Funds are handled with two-person accountability under the oversight of the Church Board, and misuse is a spiritual breach.

Restoration over condemnation

We hold those who fall. The aim is to repair the heart-home, not to demolish the person. Galatians 6:1

The discipline of unity

When a member or leader causes division, the Church Board and Elders step in following Matthew 18 to restore unity.

Respect in ministry

Every department head treats volunteers and the congregation with respect, knowing the gift is from God but the person belongs to God.

Giving

The tithe is the Lord’s, 10% of increase. We call it the prophetic rent for living on the Earth. Offerings are the seed for the future.

  • A seed of loveWe give because we love God.
  • An act of unityWe share the financial weight of the ministry together.
  • A step toward miraclesWe believe in the supernatural multiplication of resources.

We do not use guilt or manipulation. Giving is a free response of love. 2 Corinthians 9:7

Storehouse principle. Tithes are recorded by at least two witnesses.

Allocation. Funds are divided between Gospel advancement, benevolence and care, and operations.

Confidentiality. Individual giving is treated with the highest respect and privacy.

Questions people ask

Where is the church?

We are in Techiman-Jerusalem, Ghana, near Dr. Boateng Hospital. Use the Google Maps link in the Visit us section for directions.

When are the services?

Prophetic Morning Service on Sundays, 8:00 AM to 1:00 PM, and Evening Services on Tuesdays and Thursdays, 7:00 PM to 9:00 PM.

Am I expected to give?

No. Giving is a free response of love, never guilt or pressure. “Each one must give as he has decided in his heart.” 2 Corinthians 9:7

How can I request prayer?

Use the prayer request form in the Visit us section, or message us on WhatsApp. Our prayer team will stand with you.

How do I give or follow my giving?

Use the mobile money and bank details in the Giving section. Members can also record and follow their giving in the member portal.

How do I become part of the church family?

Visit us on any service day and speak with a leader, or send us a message on WhatsApp. We will help you find your place.
